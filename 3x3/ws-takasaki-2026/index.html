What drives it

Four Factors

Shooting, rebounding, getting to the line and taking care of the ball — at both ends. Every factor is scored against the rest of this tournament and signed so that positive is good, then the net factors are added up. That total is the projection; the last columns check it against where each team finished.

Both ends

Team Efficiency

Points scored and allowed per 100 possessions. 3x3 teams play at different paces, so raw totals flatter the faster sides. Top right is good at both ends.
